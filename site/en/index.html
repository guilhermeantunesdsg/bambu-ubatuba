FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an apartment?

Just message us on WhatsApp with your dates and number of guests. We'll confirm availability and arrange everything directly. You can also book through Airbnb.

How many people does each apartment sleep?

Up to 6 guests: a suite with a queen bed, a bedroom with two single beds and a sofa bed in the living room.

Are the apartments close to the beach?

Yes! Both buildings are right on the beach. The Bambu apartments face Praia Grande with an ocean view.

Is there air conditioning and Wi-Fi?

Yes. The apartments have air conditioning in the bedrooms and living room, plus high-speed Wi-Fi.

Are bed linens and towels included?

Bed linens, towels, toiletries and cleaning supplies are not included. Message us on WhatsApp if you have any questions.

Is there parking?

Yes. The Bambu apartments have 1 parking space and Reserva DNA has 2. Very large vehicles, such as Dodge RAM pickups, do not fit.

Can I bring my pet?

Sorry, pets are not allowed in our apartments.

Where should I stay in Ubatuba?

Praia Grande is one of the best areas to stay in Ubatuba: close to downtown, with waterfront kiosks, restaurants and shops, and easy access to beaches such as Toninhas and Enseada. Both buildings are right on the beach.

Are meals or full board included?

No. The apartments are in residential buildings, so we offer vacation rentals only, with access to all the leisure areas.

Do the apartments have beach gear?

Yes. Every apartment has beach gear guests can use during their stay: cooler, chairs, beach cart and umbrella.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2 pm and check-out is by 10 am.

Shall we plan your trip to Ubatuba?

The best dates book up fast, especially in high season and on holidays. Message us and secure your apartment.
